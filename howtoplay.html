THE DETAILS

Know your game.

GOOD TO KNOW
01

What is a level?

Each level adds exactly one new color. Only that new color flashes; your pattern keeps every earlier color.

02

How does scoring work?

Your score is the current level reached. The personal best is the highest level you have reached in a game.

03

What if I miss?

A wrong color or an out-of-order tap ends the game immediately. The final score and best score appear together.

04

How is my record saved?

Your personal best updates as soon as you reach a new level and stays saved in this browser.
